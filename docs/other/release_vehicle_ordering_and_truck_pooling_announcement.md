# 🌲 Automatic Forestry Designations — Vehicle Ordering & Truck Pooling

We’re excited to announce two major quality-of-life features for **Kayser's Automatic Forestry Designations**: **Vehicle Ordering** and **Truck Pooling**!

Together, these features transform how you manage forestry logistics, allowing you to expand your logging fleet with a single click and keep your tree harvesters working at peak efficiency without constant micro-management.

---

## 🏗️ Vehicle Ordering — Direct Construction from Towers

No more manually searching for an open Vehicle Depot every time your logging operation needs another tree harvester or planter!

- **One-Click Ordering**: Order vehicle construction directly from the Forestry Tower’s vehicle assignment section.
- **Smart Depot Selection**: Automatically selects the nearest eligible Vehicle Depot to build your requested vehicle.
- **Pre-Assignment**: Ordered vehicles are reserved for the tower during construction and automatically assign themselves the moment they roll off the assembly line.
- **Depot Highlighting & Shortcuts**: View enqueued vehicle progress with gold card highlights, pan directly to the building depot, or use `Shift + Alt + Click` to quickly order or cancel vehicles without confirmation prompts.

---

## 🚚 Truck Pooling — Automated Fleet Balancing

Managing vehicle assignments across individual tree harvesters in a large forestry sector can quickly become tedious. **Truck Pooling** centralizes vehicle management at the Forestry Tower level.

- **Tower-Level Fleet Management**: Assign vehicles directly to the Forestry Tower. The mod virtually pools all assigned trucks and dynamically distributes them among active tree harvesters.
- **Smart Rebalancing**: Allocation priority automatically favors larger harvesters and higher-capacity vehicles.
- **Dynamic & Seamless**: Pausing, unpausing, or modifying harvesters instantly triggers a stability-preserving rebalance without disrupting active logging work.
- **Clear Harvester Feedback**: Manual `+` and `-` buttons on individual harvester inspectors work as normal, but feature amber warning tooltips reminding you that Truck Pooling is enabled and assigned trucks may be immediately redistributed by the tower.
- **Fully Configurable & Persistent**: Toggle Truck Pooling on or off per tower or globally in Mod Settings. All settings persist seamlessly in your save file.
